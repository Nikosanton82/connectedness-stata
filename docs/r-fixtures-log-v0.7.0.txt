===========================================================================
The fixtures in test/fixtures (R_<case>_*.csv) for the DCC-GARCH cases below were
written by test/r/make_fixtures_ext.R with the ConnectednessApproach 1.0.4 sources
(CRAN release), rugarch 1.5-6 and rmgarch 1.4-3 under R 4.3.3 on Linux. The same
script was then run with the CRAN packages installed in R 4.5.2 on Windows (rugarch
1.5-3, rmgarch 1.3-9). Its full output follows (progress bars removed), and the tables
at the end compare the two sets of DCC-GARCH fixtures. The other cases are compared
in r-fixtures-log-v0.4.0.txt, r-fixtures-log-v0.5.0.txt and r-fixtures-log-v0.6.0.txt.

The DCC-GARCH model is estimated numerically by rmgarch's solnp optimizer, and its
estimates depend on the optimizer's stopping point: the two R installations, with
different rugarch and rmgarch versions, differ by up to 7.2e-7 in the estimates and
6.1e-5 in the measures. The certification script therefore checks Stata twice: with
R's estimates entered through dccparams(), where the measures agree to 1.9e-12, and
with Stata's own estimates, which agree with R's to 5.3e-6 (see
certification-log-v0.7.0.txt).
